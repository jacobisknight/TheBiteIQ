Apps that support account creation must also offer account deletion to give users more control of the data they've shared while using an app.Follow these requirements when updating an app to support account deletion:- Only offering to temporarily deactivate or disable an account is insufficient.- If users need to visit a website to finish deleting their account, include a link directly to the website page where they can complete the process.- Apps may include confirmation steps to prevent users from accidentally deleting their account.
